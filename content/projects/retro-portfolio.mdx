A love letter to late-90s terminals and to the agencies that keep WebGL interesting.

## Tech

- One persistent `<Canvas>` that survives route changes.
- Scroll progress drives scene crossfades at 60fps on a MacBook Air M1.
- Audio is off by default, fades in over 1.2s on user toggle, persists across reloads.
- JS bundle sits at ~230KB gzipped; Lighthouse 96 / 99 / 100 / 100.

## What I learned

- Instanced meshes + one shader beat dozens of materials by ~3ms/frame.
- `mix-blend-mode: difference` on the cursor is free magic.
- Lenis + GSAP ticker is the cleanest smooth-scroll setup I've tried.
