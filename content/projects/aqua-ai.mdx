Aqua-AI ingests time-series data from low-cost IoT probes and fuses it with Sentinel-2
imagery to forecast turbidity, pH, and heavy-metal contamination 24 hours ahead.

## Problem

Municipalities in northern India only test water weekly. Failures propagate faster than that.

## Approach

- **Edge**: ESP32 with pH, TDS, and turbidity probes, sampling every 90s.
- **Ingest**: FastAPI + TimescaleDB.
- **Model**: A gated 1D-CNN over the last 6h of probe data, concatenated with latent
  features from a ResNet-18 fine-tuned on Sentinel-2 patches.
- **Delivery**: Next.js dashboard with per-ward risk scores and alert webhooks.

## Outcome

- 0.89 ROC-AUC on a held-out month from Delhi's Najafgarh drain cluster.
- 14 hours median lead time on contamination events.
